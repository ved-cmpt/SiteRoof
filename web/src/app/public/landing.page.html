Sheet 04 · ERP handoff

Approved once, delivered once, even when the ERP is down

Approving a request writes the decision, the audit entry and an outbox message in one database transaction. A background worker delivers the message to a self-hosted mock ERP with an idempotency key.

  1. 01 The demo ERP rejects the first attempt with 503.
  2. 02 The worker retries with exponential backoff, at most five attempts.
  3. 03 The ERP stores one requisition per key; the reference comes back to SiteRoof.

The Approvals screen shows each transmission move from queued to retrying to acknowledged.

Sheet 06 · About

Why this exists

SiteRoof is an independent portfolio project, built while applying for a software developer student role at a general contractor in Edmonton. It shows, with working code, the internal software construction teams rely on: secure web apps, clear APIs, careful relational data, reliable integrations and tests that check the business rules.

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any company. Every project, person and record in it is fictional.
